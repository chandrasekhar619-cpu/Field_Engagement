Financial Rules for the Next Generation

5 essential money habits to start early

Starting Financial Planning Early Building financial discipline early is the greatest headstart you can give your child. Teaching them how money works today ensures they make smart, independent choices tomorrow.

1. Spending Money Means Saying "No" to Something Else

Spending Rs. 500 on a snack today means that same Rs. 500 cannot be used for another goal tomorrow. Every purchase has an opportunity cost.

Daily ActionPause before an impulse buy and name one bigger goal you are choosing instead.

2. Divide Money into 3 Jars

Split every incoming amount into three buckets: Spend (now), Save (later), and Give (for others).

Daily ActionCreate 3 jars today and split pocket money as soon as it comes in.

3. Needs vs. Wants

A need is essential (food, water, school basics). A want is optional (toys, treats, extra game passes).

Daily ActionPick 3 things at home that are needs and 3 that are wants.

4. Money Represents Time and Effort

Money does not appear automatically. It comes from time, energy, and skills applied through work.

Daily ActionLink one household task to effort and value to understand how earning works.

5. Save Before You Spend

The most effective habit is to move savings first and spend what remains, not the other way around.

Daily ActionAs soon as pocket money arrives, put a part into savings before spending anything.
