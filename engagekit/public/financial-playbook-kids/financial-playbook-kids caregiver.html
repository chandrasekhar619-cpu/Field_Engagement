Financial Rules for the Next Generation

Caregiver Playbook: Gentle money habits with lasting impact

Teach Money with Care and ConsistencySimple, kind money lessons early can help children grow into confident and responsible adults. This guide makes those lessons easy to start at home.

1. Spending One Thing Means Letting Go of Another

Every spending choice comes with another choice we postpone.

Daily ActionAsk: what future goal are we protecting by waiting today?

2. Use 3 Jars with Purpose

Spend, save, and give help children build balance and awareness.

Daily ActionSplit every pocket money amount into 3 jars.

3. Understand Needs and Wants

Needs support life and safety. Wants are optional and can be planned.

Daily ActionPick examples together from daily life.

4. Money Reflects Time and Hard Work

Children value money better when they understand effort behind earning.

Daily ActionDiscuss one task and the work it takes to complete.

5. Save First, Then Spend

Saving first helps build confidence and creates healthy long-term habits.

Daily ActionMove savings first whenever money comes in.
